Pricing

Practitioner development offers three distinct pathways, each with a defined practice authorization.

OEI uses Researcher and Practitioner roles. Understand how the roles work together.

The roles use separate competency standards. The 60% training and assessment payment is due upfront to begin. It is non-refundable. Passing the applicable assessment earns certification and authorization for that pathway. The remaining 40% is due only after the candidate passes and earns authorization. It confirms the authorization already earned. It is not due simply because training is complete or an assessment has been attempted.

Training and authorization prices

Training develops role-specific capability. Passing the applicable assessment earns certification and pathway authorization. Authorization sets the permitted scope and capacity. The remaining 40% confirms the authorization already earned.

Company-Sponsored

$10,000 USD total

The $10,000 package includes one Practitioner training and assessment process and one separate Researcher training and assessment process for two different people, plus one Practitioner certification and authorization for internal OEI capability.

$6,000 covers the two training and assessment processes
$4,000 covers Practitioner certification and authorization, payable after the Practitioner passes

If the Practitioner passes but the Researcher fails, the $4,000 is still due for Practitioner authorization. The company then has an authorized Practitioner without a qualified Researcher. The Researcher may perform research functions with a Practitioner but cannot independently complete an OEI engagement. The Practitioner may conduct full OEI engagements and Focused Investigations within the sponsoring organization. Neither role authorizes external client work or Institute representation. Each certification belongs to its individual holder.

If the Researcher does not pass but the Practitioner does, the company may train one additional Researcher candidate for a flat $3,000. This covers one Researcher training process and assessment. It does not include additional Practitioner training. The qualified Researcher works with the company’s already certified and authorized Practitioner.

Annual renewal: $500 per internally employed company-sponsored Practitioner, per year. No renewal fee applies to Researchers.

OEI Native

$15,000 USD total

Qualify a Practitioner to represent the Institute and deliver engagements to Institute clients under its standards and delivery arrangements. The Institute may train or assign Researchers separately.

$9,000 Practitioner training and assessment
$6,000 authorization confirmation, payable only after passing and gaining authorization

Training and assessment qualify the candidate for the Practitioner role. Passing earns certification and OEI Native authorization. Payment alone does not guarantee a passing result or a client assignment.

Annual renewal: $750 per year.

If a candidate fails the first assessment, one retake is available by paying the full 60% training and assessment amount again. Each candidate may attempt assessment twice in total. The non-refundable 60% payments are $12,000 for Company-Sponsored, $14,400 for Independent Consultant, and $18,000 for OEI Native if the candidate fails both attempts. The 40% is not due unless the candidate passes and gains certification and authorization. Read the full retake terms.

Annual authorization renewal

Renewal is 5% of the applicable pathway price. The first year of renewal is waived after certification. Renewal sustains active authorization and supports alignment with the current OEI methodology through Institute-provided Methodology Updates.

Company-sponsored renewal is $500 per internally employed Practitioner, not per company. Researchers have no renewal fee. If a practitioner upgrades pathways, the renewal rate changes immediately to 5% of the new pathway price. For example, company-sponsored Practitioner authorization changes from $500 to $600 per year after an independent upgrade, or to $750 per year after an OEI Native upgrade. The first renewal year is waived after certification. Read the renewal, lapse, and update rules.

Upgrading from company-sponsored authorization

If a Company-Sponsored Practitioner leaves, the company stops paying renewal and their company-specific authorization ends. The Practitioner retains certification. The now former employee may personally pay $2,000 for independent authorization, pursue the OEI Native pathway for $7,500 subject to applicable requirements, or allow authorization to lapse.

How AI Enablement is scoped ↓

Full OEI Native engagement payments

Full OEI Native engagements are paid through four milestone-based installments, not hourly billing. Each installment is due upon delivery of its corresponding deliverable. The milestones are concrete work products, not arbitrary calendar dates:

  1. The 4-day OEI Diagnosis record
  2. The audit findings
  3. The implementation
  4. The validated results

Client-caused delays

Before the 4-day OEI Diagnosis begins, delays caused by missing access, evidence, or other prerequisites are documented in the diagnostic report, along with resulting limits on confidence in affected findings. A pre-diagnosis delay does not automatically create a financial penalty or forfeiture.

After the OEI Diagnosis, client-caused delays, including delays affecting audit findings or implementation, extend the engagement term under the client’s responsibility. The engagement does not automatically terminate when information, access, decisions, or other inputs are delayed. A pause may last a maximum of three months, measured from the date the delay begins. After that, continuation under the original terms is not guaranteed. The client must coordinate with the Institute to resume work. Remaining milestones may be re-quoted or handled under other applicable terms. The engagement is scoped around effort and deliverables, not a guaranteed completion date.

Optional intervention layer

AI Enablement: scoped from OEI findings

AI Enablement can be added to either OEI pathway when the investigation identifies a worthwhile opportunity. It is scoped according to the intervention the OEI work actually supports; it is not an automatic part of every engagement.

OEI begins with evidence, determines whether AI is appropriate, and tests any intervention against operational outcomes. The right conclusion may be not to proceed with AI.
