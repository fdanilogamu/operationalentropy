Ways to Engage with the OEI Institute

The OEI Institute governs the Operational Entropy Index methodology and its practice standards. Choose the way of working that fits your organization’s needs, from building internal capability to focused investigation or Institute-led delivery.

Three distinct practice relationships

OEI application uses two complementary roles: Researcher and Practitioner. Certification and authorization are role-specific. Learn how the roles work together.

Company-sponsored

Company-Sponsored pathway

Build internal capability by training two different people: one Practitioner and one Researcher. Their role-specific authorizations are scoped to the sponsoring organization.

Independent practice

Independent consultant pathway

Train one individual for both Researcher and Practitioner functions to serve multiple clients, subject to meeting the requirements for both roles. Independent authorization does not permit Institute representation.

Institute delivery

OEI Native

Qualify a Practitioner to represent the OEI Institute and deliver engagements under its standards. Researchers may be trained or assigned separately by the Institute.

Training, certification, and authorization

The $10,000 Company-Sponsored pathway includes one Practitioner training and assessment process and one separate Researcher training and assessment process for two different people, plus one Practitioner authorization. The Researcher works with the Practitioner by preparing and handing off the analysis-ready record. If the Researcher fails but the Practitioner passes, the $4,000 Practitioner authorization payment remains due. The company may train one additional Researcher candidate for $3,000.

Training develops role-specific competence. Passing the assessment earns certification and authorization for the applicable pathway. The remaining 40% payment confirms the authorization already earned and is due only after passing. The initial 60% training and assessment payment is non-refundable. See the training, assessment, retake, and authorization terms.

Annual renewal is 5% of the pathway price: $500 per internally employed company-sponsored Practitioner per year, $600 per year for independent consultants, and $750 per year for OEI Native practitioners. Researchers have no renewal fee. The first renewal year is waived after certification. Learn about renewal, upgrades, and lapsed authorization.

AI Enablement through OEI

AI is considered after the operating condition is understood. OEI work may identify an opportunity for an AI intervention, or conclude that AI is not the right response.

Understand

Examine the operating condition

Investigate the relevant workflow and establish the baseline before considering an AI intervention.

Determine

Scope what the evidence supports

Assess whether AI is suitable and select or design an intervention only when findings warrant it.

Apply and validate

Test against the intended outcome

Build and test the capability where appropriate, verify results, and enable the organization to use and maintain it.

AI Enablement is an optional intervention within OEI work. Findings may support AI, another operational change, or a decision not to use AI.
