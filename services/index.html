OEI Institute

Practitioner development pathways

The OEI Institute governs the Operational Entropy Index methodology and its practice standards. Choose a pathway based on where and on whose behalf you intend to apply OEI.

Three distinct practice relationships

OEI application uses two complementary roles: Researcher and Practitioner. Certification and authorization are role-specific. Learn how the roles work together.

Company-sponsored

Company-sponsored pathway

Build internal capability by training two different people: one Practitioner and one Researcher. Their role-specific authorizations are scoped to the sponsoring organization.

Independent practice

Independent consultant pathway

Train one individual for both Researcher and Practitioner functions to serve multiple clients, subject to meeting the requirements for both roles. Independent authorization does not permit Institute representation.

Institute delivery

OEI Native practitioner

Qualify an Practitioner to represent the OEI Institute and deliver engagements under its standards. Researchers may be trained or assigned separately by the Institute.

Training, certification, and authorization

Training develops role-specific competence. Certification records competence for that role. Authorization defines where and in what capacity the person may practice. Passing an assessment is required; payment does not guarantee certification. See the training and authorization pathways.

Annual renewal is 5% of the pathway price: $500 per internally employed company-sponsored Practitioner per year, $600 per year for independent consultants, and $750 per year for OEI Native practitioners. Researchers have no renewal fee. The first renewal year is waived after certification. Learn about renewal, upgrades, and lapsed authorization.

Start with a Focused Investigation

A Focused Investigation is a bounded way to examine a specific operational condition without committing to a broader OEI engagement. Each includes a 4-Day OEI Diagnosis and can be an appropriate entry point when a particular domain needs attention. Scope and effort vary with the work and judgment required.

Current public range: $1,000–$4,000 USD.

  • Founder Absence Simulation
  • Institutional Memory Recovery Sprint
  • Workflow Momentum Analysis
  • Operational Stack Review
  • Handoff Failure Analysis

AI Enablement through OEI

AI is considered after the operating condition is understood. OEI work may identify an opportunity for an AI intervention—or conclude that AI is not the right response.

Understand the operating condition→Determine the warranted intervention→Use AI where it actually helps

When appropriate, AI Enablement through OEI investigates the operating condition, determines whether AI is suitable, selects or designs an intervention, builds and tests the capability, verifies results against the intended operational outcome, and enables the organization to use and maintain it. AI Enablement is an optional intervention layer within OEI work, not an unrelated AI consulting product or a replacement for OEI methodology.
