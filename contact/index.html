If follow-up is appropriate

The next step depends on the operational question, the evidence available, and the current Institute delivery structure. Scope, access, and timing can be discussed directly.

  1. Clarify the operational question. Identify the problem, the relevant people and systems, and the evidence available for review.
  2. Agree the scope. Determine what can be investigated and what form the work should take under the current Institute framework.
  3. Decide the next step. Confirm timing, access, and any documentation needed before work begins.
