Common signs that your company needs this

If any of these resonate, organizational entropy is silently eroding your growth.

Why should this worry me?

Leadership bottleneck

Is the company operationally hostage to one person's presence? Founders handling a high percentage of critical decisions slows the whole machine.

Process breakdown

Processes break down despite strong people. Work stalls between departments or approvals become invisible chokepoints.

Context loss

Teams operating with incomplete context. Coordination overhead grows with headcount, meetings compensate for unclear systems.

Tool mess

Systems exist but aren't used consistently or fit for purpose. Training is slow and onboarding is difficult every time.

Handoff failures

Responsibilities and context don't transfer cleanly between teams. Single points of failure are hiding inside everyday workflows.

Reliability decline

Execution reliability declines despite team effort. Work takes longer, decisions get reversed, rework happens constantly.

The 5 pillars

The Operational Entropy Index examines operational entropy across five pillars. Each one represents a common source of strain as organizations grow.

Founder Dependency

Is the company operationally hostage to one person's presence? When the founder has to approve everything, the company can't scale.

Knowledge Logistics

Workflow Velocity

How the OEI process works

A full OEI Native engagement moves through four concrete deliverables. Each milestone payment is due when its corresponding deliverable is delivered. Scope and elapsed time depend on the work and the inputs available.

01

4-day OEI Diagnosis record

Research and analysis establish a traceable account of the operating conditions and evidence.

02

Audit findings

Interpret the evidence and present supported findings about the operational conditions.

03

Implementation

Carry out the scoped changes supported by the findings.

04

Validated results

Review outcomes against the intended operational changes.
