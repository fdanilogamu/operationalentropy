## Change

OEI introduces a browser-based assessment that routes visitors toward a minimum engagement path based on operational friction and primary pain. It gives visitors a recommendation without requiring contact details.

## Notes

This establishes self-guided engagement routing as part of the OEI customer journey. Later records document the separation of the main engagement and focused-investigation paths.
