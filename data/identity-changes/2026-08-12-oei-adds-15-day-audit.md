## Change

OEI audit work is offered at two depths. The 15-Day Audit is a shorter option for simpler analyses with fewer areas to monitor; the 30-Day Audit remains the comprehensive option for broader scope or deeper analysis.

## Notes

The 15-Day Audit baseline is $8,000 for companies under $5M in revenue. The 30-Day Audit baseline remains $15,000. Both require an initial diagnosis and scale with company revenue.
