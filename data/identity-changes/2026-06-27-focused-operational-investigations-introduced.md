## Change

OEI introduces five Focused Operational Investigations for companies that already know where operational friction exists: Founder Absence Simulation, Institutional Memory Recovery Sprint, Workflow Momentum Analysis, Operational Stack Review, and Handoff Failure Analysis.

## Notes

Focused investigations apply OEI methodology to one operational domain rather than requiring a comprehensive assessment. The initial overview lists a typical investment of $500 to $2,000 USD.
