## Change

The Operational Entropy Index is publicly defined as a proprietary diagnostic tool and structured intervention process for identifying, measuring, and reducing organizational drag across five dimensions: Founder Dependency, Knowledge Logistics, Workflow Velocity, Tool Discipline, and Handoff Integrity.
