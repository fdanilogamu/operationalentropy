## Change

Entropy Compatible Hiring Version 0.1.3 adds Existing Employee Contribution Mapping as a distinct module alongside its candidate-hiring instruments. The module compares an employee's reflection with separate manager observations across the ten OEI archetypes, identifies convergence, discrepancy, and missing evidence, and recommends a provisional archetype for validation through a bounded work exercise.

## Notes

This broadens ECH from observing candidate behavior during hiring to exploring potential entropy-reducing contributions among current employees. Hiring and existing-employee sessions remain separate. Contribution Mapping is explicitly exploratory rather than evaluative: its results are provisional contribution hypotheses, not performance ratings, validated cutoffs, or permanent employee labels.
