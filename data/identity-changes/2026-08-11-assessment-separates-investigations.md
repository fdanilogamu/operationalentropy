## Change

The OEI Engagement Path Assessment now distinguishes between companies that need help identifying the right engagement and companies that already know which operational constraints are present. Known issues route through a separate focused-investigation path, while broader or uncertain conditions continue through the main engagement path.

## Notes

One or two clearly identified constraints produce a 4-Day Diagnosis followed by the relevant focused investigation. Three or more selected constraints return the user to the broader engagement path.
