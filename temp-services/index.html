OEI Institute

Current pathways into OEI

The OEI Institute governs the Operational Entropy Index methodology. Organizations can access and develop that capability in different ways, depending on the work they need and the capability they want to build.

Ways to work with OEI

There are two decisions behind a broader OEI pathway: who develops and applies the OEI capability, and whether an AI intervention is warranted by the findings. AI Enablement can be added where useful; it is not required to apply OEI and is not an OEI pillar.

How OEI is applied

Build internal capability
Develop your organization’s own OEI capability through staff training and certification as Institute pathways become available.

Work with an OEI practitioner
Qualified OEI practitioners perform the diagnosis and methodology application for your organization.

Optional intervention

Without AI Enablement
Apply OEI to understand the operating condition and determine an appropriate response.

With AI Enablement
Include AI Enablement when OEI findings identify a worthwhile AI opportunity.

Start with a Focused Investigation

A Focused Investigation is a bounded way to examine a specific operational condition without committing to a broader OEI engagement. It can be an appropriate entry point when a particular domain needs attention. Scope and effort vary with the work and judgment required.

Current public range: $1,000–$4,000 USD.

  • Founder Absence Simulation
  • Institutional Memory Recovery Sprint
  • Workflow Momentum Analysis
  • Operational Stack Review
  • Handoff Failure Analysis

AI Enablement through OEI

AI is considered after the operating condition is understood. OEI work may identify an opportunity for an AI intervention—or conclude that AI is not the right response.

Understand the operating condition→Determine the warranted intervention→Use AI where it actually helps

When appropriate, AI Enablement through OEI investigates the operating condition, determines whether AI is suitable, selects or designs an intervention, builds and tests the capability, verifies results against the intended operational outcome, and enables the organization to use and maintain it. AI Enablement is an optional intervention layer within OEI work, not an unrelated AI consulting product or a replacement for OEI methodology.
