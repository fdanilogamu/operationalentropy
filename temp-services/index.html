Current ways to apply OEI

The OEI Institute is organizing how applications of the Operational Entropy Index and their delivery fit together. Two current paths are available to explore; the broader services, practitioner-led delivery, and commercial structure are still being developed.

Focused Investigations

When an operational problem is already visible, a Focused Investigation applies OEI methodology to a single operational domain to investigate root causes and the conditions shaping the work.

Existing investigations address areas such as founder dependency, institutional memory, workflow momentum, operational tools, and handoffs. They offer a focused way to examine a known area of operational friction; they are distinct from a comprehensive examination across the OEI pillars.

Explore Focused Investigations

AI Enablement Through OEI

AI Enablement helps growing teams investigate the work first, then build and test an AI capability only where it can credibly reduce operational drag. It is a focused application of the OEI framework, supporting practical AI literacy, investigation of a bounded operating condition, and testing whether a governed AI capability can materially improve it.

Pricing has not yet been established or published. It is discussed as part of determining the appropriate engagement.

The wider structure is still taking shape

The two paths above are current applications of OEI, not a finalized services catalog. How broader engagements, practitioner-led work, and other applied tools fit together—and the wider commercial structure—are still being organized. Practitioner certification is also in development.
